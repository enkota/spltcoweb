Game modes

Play four game modes with your friends. From classic prop hunt to VIP prop defence.

Infection

Classic prop hunt. Props respawn as hunters and those who successfully hide, win.

Respawn as hunter

Last Stand

Get caught and you're out until the next round. Swap sides each round in a best-of series.

No respawns

Rush

Ticket based prop count. Hunters must deplete tickets before the time runs out.

Respawn tickets

VIP

Random props are picked as VIP and you must defend from hunters to win in a best-of series.

Unlimited respawns
