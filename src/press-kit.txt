SPLATICO
SURVIVE THE HUNT

Multiplayer voxel prop hunt.

Props disguise themselves as objects on the map and hide. Hunters search with paint guns and air blasts. Props can use decoys, disguises, taunts, and abilities to survive the hunt.

FEATURES
- Online multiplayer over Steam.
- Four modes: Infection, Last Stand, Rush, and VIP.
- Chaos events change the conditions during a round.
- Character customization with hats, faces, face colors, and emotes.
- Built-in voxel map editor with custom block, prop, asset, and decal imports.
- Learn about creating custom maps: https://splatico.fandom.com/wiki/Getting_Started
- Steam Workshop map sharing with custom content included.
